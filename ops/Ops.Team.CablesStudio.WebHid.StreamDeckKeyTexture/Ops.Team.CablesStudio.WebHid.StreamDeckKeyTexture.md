# Ops.Team.CablesStudio.WebHid.StreamDeckKeyTexture

Captures a WebGL texture, resizes and flips it, and sends it to a specific button display on a Stream Deck using WebHID.

## Compatibility
Works seamlessly with both `Ops.Team.CablesStudio.WebHid.StreamDeck` and `Ops.Team.CablesStudio.WebHid.ExternalStreamDeck`.
