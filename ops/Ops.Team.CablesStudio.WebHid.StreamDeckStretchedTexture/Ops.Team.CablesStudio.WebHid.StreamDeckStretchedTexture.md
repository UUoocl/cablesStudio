# Ops.Team.CablesStudio.WebHid.StreamDeckStretchedTexture

Captures a WebGL texture, resizes and flips it to fit the overall button grid display matrix of a Stream Deck, and uploads it using WebHID.

## Compatibility
Works seamlessly with both `Ops.Team.CablesStudio.WebHid.StreamDeck` and `Ops.Team.CablesStudio.WebHid.ExternalStreamDeck`.
